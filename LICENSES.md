# Licensing and attribution

The recovered eT source snapshot retains its original author notices and GNU General Public License, version 3. Its complete license text is included at `vendor/eT-LICENSE` and inside the source archive. The eT modifications are distributed as source patches against the identified snapshot.

i-PI, libcint, optional LibXC, and Python dependencies retain their respective upstream licenses and citation requirements. The installer fetches the pinned upstream dependencies; this repository does not assign new terms to them.

The recovered interface/workflow files did not contain a separate top-level license grant. This preparation preserves their status and does not silently assign a license or change copyright ownership. Before a formal public software release, the maintainers should select and record the license for their own contributions. GPL-3.0 is a candidate consistent with the bundled eT source; confirm the intended scope with all contribution owners.

The software and scientific-method author lists, contact address, citation metadata and publication identifiers should be supplied by the maintainers. This package does not invent coauthors or a DOI.
